The property that makes it work is also the constraint

On a public ledger, “verifiable by anyone” and “readable by anyone” are the same thing.

Holdings are public

A six-figure bottle is permanently attributable to an address, and chain analysis routinely links addresses to people. The most privacy-sensitive customers in the portfolio are the most exposed.

Allocation strategy is public

Mint patterns, release sizes, regional distribution, and how fast a drop clears are readable by any competitor, auctioneer, or reseller with a block explorer.

The consumer signal is shared

The open-and-claim data used for allocation and marketing insight is not proprietary once it is onchain. Suntory pays to generate it; the secondary market reads it for free.

It caps what comes next

Casks, distributor settlement, supplier terms, and sustainability data cannot be published to a transparent ledger. Without confidentiality, the programme stops at collectibles.

Where this creates value

Tokenized stock
Casks and aging inventory

Fractional cask ownership and warehouse receipts. Existence and total supply are provable to holders and lenders; positions, valuations, and financing terms stay private.

Settlement
Distributor and B2B flows

Onchain settlement with distributors and importers without publishing volumes, pricing tiers, or regional margins — disclosable to auditors and tax authorities on request.

Secondary market
Ownership and resale

Continuous provenance and brand royalties across resale, with holder identity and price history confidential instead of broadcast to the auction market.

Loyalty
One programme, many brands

Points, access, and redeemable experiences across the portfolio. The outstanding liability is provably reserved for finance; member balances and promo economics are not public.

Provenance
Upstream supply chain

Lot-level chain of custody for barley, oak, agave, tea, and packaging. Certification is verifiable to buyers and retailers; supplier identity, volumes, and terms are not exposed.

Sustainability
Water claims, provable

Replenishment and Scope 3 claims verifiable to regulators, retailers, and auditors without publishing plant-level operational data in water-stressed regions.

What is platform, and what is a build

Worth being precise before anything gets scoped.

Available today
Confidential asset infrastructure

Issuance and redemption against reserves, confidential transfers with policy limits, participation and KYC gating, proof of reserves, scoped and logged disclosure, immutable audit log, Console, APIs and webhooks, and an optional branded wallet.

Scoped alongside the platform
Provenance and attestations

Item-level physical traceability and confidential claims — sustainability data, supplier credentials, duty and movement records — sit next to the platform rather than inside it. Delivered as scoped work by a forward-deployed engineer, against the same policy and audit model.

Stays with Suntory
Custody, brand, and compliance

Ryle is a technology provider, not a custodian: it holds no reserves, assets, or wallet keys. The existing custody relationship, the NFC and authenticity stack, the brands, the customer relationships, and the regulatory programme all remain Suntory's.
