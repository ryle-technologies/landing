The challenge

It isn’t just software: it’s time, complexity, and organizational capacity.

Time to market

A typical internal build takes 18–24 months. Meanwhile the use case loses priority, budget dilutes, and the competitive window closes.

Technical complexity

Networks, wallets, identity, policy, reporting, and privacy all have to fit. Each layer adds integration risk and operational debt.

Teams and vendors

You need a multidisciplinary team that is hard to hire and retain — and, in practice, multiple vendors that don’t share one model. The result: friction, rework, and dependency.

Control and exposure

Operating on public infrastructure demands institutional control: who sees what, under which rules, with what audit trail. Full transparency is not acceptable for the business.
