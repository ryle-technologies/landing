Features
  • Asset lifecycle — launch, mint, redeem, pause, wind down
  • Supply and reserve reconciliation
  • Policy controls — holders, caps, allowlists, pauses
  • APIs & webhooks for product embedding
  • Settlement on Ethereum, Polygon, Base, and more
Benefits
  • Launch without assembling a chain stack
  • One model across Console and APIs
  • Sandbox and live share the same capabilities
  • Faster path from pilot to production
  • Institutional control without custody through us

Confidentiality Onchain

Encrypted balances and transfers on public chains — without giving up auditability.

Product 03
Private by default. Visible by policy. Auditable always.

One product in the suite — layer confidentiality on a new asset, an existing one, or a third-party asset you support.

Features
  • Encrypted balances, amounts, and counterparties
  • Zero-knowledge proofs of transfer validity
  • Selective disclosure — scoped, timed, logged
  • Proof of reserves without holder exposure
  • Immutable, attributed audit log
Benefits
  • Public rails without public financial exposure
  • Keep EVM liquidity and interoperability
  • Satisfy auditors without publishing the ledger
  • Private by default, visible by policy
  • Adopt only when the use case requires it
